## Two terms the spec sized as small and were not

**1. The Farmstead's `boost: { provisions: 0.03 }` is worth ×4.40, not 3%.** At the run's own counts (60 Farmsteads, pop 201), provisions measure 337.63/s with it and 76.65/s without. The per-copy figure is Kittens' Aqueduct figure exactly — but Kittens' aqueduct is a distinct mid-game building and RR put the term on the **cheapest starter building at ratio 1.12**, of which the bot builds sixty. `boosts.provisions` has no `BOOST_LIMIT` entry, so it is unbounded. Shipped as specified (explicit pass condition) and reported. **Fix is one line: bound it, or move it off the starter.**

**2. `resRatio` multiplies the NET rate and is guarded on `rates > 0`.**

```js
for (var r4 in resRatio) if (rates[r4] > 0 && resRatio[r4] !== 1) rates[r4] *= resRatio[r4];
```

So any resource running a deficit got **no bonus at all**. Hexresonance was a dead upgrade whenever mana ran negative, which at Era 3 is routine — moving it to `boosts.mana` swung a synthetic Era-3 state from −6.38 to +3.38 mana/s. **The same trap is still live for the one member `resRatio` still has**: Cultivation's +10% provisions does nothing at the moment a settlement is starving.

## What v0.50 changed

### Part 1 — the census (the table is in `CENSUS-TABLE-v0.50.txt`, generated by `census-table.mjs`)

- **`mw` is gone.** Masterwork Tools was a cross-resource job multiplier — a category with one member, which Kittens has nowhere. It is a rung in `AXE_LINE` (7 rungs, Σ3.45, ×4.45) and in the new `HOE_LINE` (2 rungs, Σ0.55, ×1.55).
- **`HOE_LINE` is new** — Kittens' `catnipJobRatio`. Same table shape as AXE/SAW so `hoeDesc()` generates its prose. `masterworkDesc()` generates from **both** lines, since it is the one upgrade in two.
- **`SAW_LINE` gains a sixth rung** (Seasoned Timberworks, out of `resRatio`): Σ1.20, Lumber Mill 0.220 per copy.
- **`resRatio` has one member**, provisions, by Jerry's ruling.
- **`global` is five categories, not seven.** `catCharts`, `catDrake` and `catSoul` collapsed into `catMeta = 1 + limitedDR(sum, META_LIMIT=1.0)`, ×1.8197 at a full stack against ×2.058. The Baron went ×2.00 → **×1.25**.

**The collapse has TWO outputs and it must stay that way.** `catMetaTransient` carries the charts term alone, so knowledge, culture, vigor and devotion take ×1.10 exactly as before and do **not** inherit the drakes or the Dragon Soul. This is the v0.47 devotion trap in a new shape; a test asserts all four measure ×1.000 with the Infernal Drake at 100 kills and the Soul owned.

## Two operational rules I broke again and you should not

- **Never `pkill -f` a pattern that matches your own shell.** `pkill -f "index-iso-p2.html"` killed the bash process running it (exit 144) and silently dropped two queued patches. This is the identical mistake documented in the v0.46 report. Kill by PID from `ps -eo pid,args`.
- **Never `sleep` past the tool timeout with background jobs running.** A 10-minute sleep hitting the timeout returned exit 143 and took a 20-minute pacing run with it. Size sleeps under the limit.
- And instrument the metrics the spec asks for **before** launching runs. The Glorious Evolution numbers were named in the spec's §2.3 and I re-ran two 2,500-year runs to get them.
